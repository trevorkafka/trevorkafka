Already a student and loving your lessons with me? Share them! When you refer a new student, you both earn a free lesson. Just email me at to let me know that one student was referred by another before the new student's first lesson takes place.

How it works

  • Your friend (from a family new to lessons with me) pays for their first two lessons at the regular rate, and their third lesson is free.
  • You (the referrer) receive one free lesson, credited once your friend has completed and paid for their first two lessons.

Important Details

  • Referral discounts can only be claimed by brand-new families. The referred student must belong to a family that has never taken paid lessons with me (this includes siblings and other household members of current or former students).
  • Let me know about the referral by email at before the new student’s first lesson.
  • The referrer’s free lesson is credited only after the referred student completes and pays for their first two lessons.
  • There is no limit on how many friends you may refer.
  • Free lessons must be used within 90 days. This applies to both the referrer and the referred student.
