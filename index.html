Frequently Asked Questions

How much do you charge for lessons?

My base rate is US$200/hour. To keep quality tutoring as accessible as possible while sustaining my practice, I offer tiered pricing. Choose the option that best fits your current circumstances:

Tier Hourly Rate
Standard TierUS$200/hour
Reduced Tier (20% off)US$160/hour
Supporter Tier (20% more)US$240/hour
  • Standard Tier: Pay the base rate of US$200/hour. Simply register as usual.
  • Reduced Tier: If a reduced rate would make it easier for you to participate, just let me know by email at and I'll set you up. No application or documentation is required.
  • Supporter Tier: If you're able to contribute a little extra, you can help make reduced-rate lessons possible for others by paying above the base rate. Register at the standard rate and let me know you'd like to pay it forward.

Whichever tier you choose, the services I provide are strictly identical across all tiers, with the same preparation, the same lessons, and the same attention. The tier only affects what you pay, never what you receive. Please choose the option that honestly fits your situation, and I won't ask for any proof of your financial circumstances. Unless I hear from you in advance of our lessons, the standard rate will apply. If a different rate would be appropriate for your own financial situation, please reach out so we can discuss.

Students who began lessons with me some time ago may carry different legacy prices. In any case, prices for lessons will never change without written notification provided in advance.

What platform do you use for your online classes?

Zoom is my preferred internet communication tool for my tutoring sessions. After logging in and registering for a tutoring session with me, you will receive a class reminder email with a Zoom link for class 36 hours in advance of your appointment start time.

Do you assign homework?

Yes, I most certainly can and am happy to! Homework assignments are available by request. They are generally not provided by default but under most circumstances are offered free of charge.

Students are often very busy, so additional tutor-assigned homework is not always appropriate, but if it would be helpful to have homework assignments from me given to you for time outside of class, please let me know, and I can provide this to you for no extra charge (as long as the amount of time it takes me to construct and review the assignments is within reason).

What is your cancellation policy?
  • No payment is due for any cancellations made 24 or more hours in advance of the class start time.
  • Full payment will be due for cancellations made less than 24 hours in advance of the class start time.

Exceptions to this policy may be made on a case-by-case basis, usually under circumstances where late schedule changes do not pose an inconvenience. The fundamental intent of these policies is so that I can ensure a reliable work schedule and work-life balance, not to create "gotcha" situations for clients. I try to be as reasonable as possible in consideration of the factors surrounding any late cancellation and I request the same of my clients.
