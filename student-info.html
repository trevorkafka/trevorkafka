Booking & Scheduling

All scheduling happens in the student portal. You can book, cancel, and see your upcoming lessons there at any time, and you can browse my next 90 days of availability before you log in to find a time that works.

How do I register for a lesson?

Log in to the student portal, open the calendar, and click an open time slot to register for it. Open slots are the times I have available; if you don't see any that work, email me and we'll figure something out.

Lessons can be booked as little as 12 hours in advance. That said, if your own schedule is tight, I'd recommend booking about three weeks ahead so you can count on getting the times you want.

How do I cancel a lesson?

Cancel in the student portal, or simply reply to your lesson reminder email to let me know. Please see the cancellation policy below regarding timing.

Can I set up a weekly recurring lesson time?

Yes, and this is the easiest way to lock in a time that works for you. Email me at and let me know which day and time you'd like, and I'll set it up on my end.

How do I sign up for lesson reminders?

Email reminders are on by default and arrive 36 hours before class with your Zoom link. You can also turn on text reminders, which arrive two hours before class: log in to the student portal, open your profile, and edit your notification settings.

Can I add my lessons to Google Calendar or iCal?

Yes. In the student portal, open the calendar and look for the subscribe or sync option, which gives you a calendar feed URL you can add to Google Calendar, iCal, Outlook, or any other calendar app. Your lessons will then appear alongside everything else in your calendar and update automatically.

What time zone does the calendar show?

Everything is shown in your own local time, so there's no conversion to do on your end. Just make sure your time zone settings in the student portal match that of your current location.

Policies

What is your cancellation policy?
  • No payment is due for any cancellations made 24 or more hours in advance of the class start time.
  • Full payment will be due for cancellations made less than 24 hours in advance of the class start time.

Exceptions to this policy may be made on a case-by-case basis, usually under circumstances where late schedule changes do not pose an inconvenience. The fundamental intent of these policies is so that I can ensure a reliable work schedule and work-life balance, not to create "gotcha" situations for clients. I try to be as reasonable as possible in consideration of the factors surrounding any late cancellation and I request the same of my clients.

How long are lessons, and can I change my default length?

Most students take 60-minute lessons, but you can request whatever length works best for you (45, 60, 75, 90 minutes, and so on). Email me at to change your default lesson length. There's more detail on the lesson lengths FAQ.

What are your rates, and are there discounts?

Current rates are listed in the pricing FAQ on my homepage. Two ways to bring the cost down:

  • Package discounts for buying 10, 20, or 40 lessons at once.
  • Referral discount when you refer a new student to me.

How do I enroll in automatic payments?

Log in to the student portal and look for the auto pay option in your billing settings. Once enrolled, invoices are paid automatically when they're issued, so there's nothing for you to remember each month. Auto pay uses online payment, so the 2.89% processing fee applies.

How do I make a one-time payment?

Your invoice email includes a direct payment link, or you can log in to the student portal and pay from your account. Zelle and paper checks don't go through the portal; just send those directly using the details in the table above.

What if I need more time to pay?

If you anticipate any payment delays or need to set up a payment plan, please just reply to your invoice email or write to me at to reach out. I'm happy to work something out.

Before & During Class

Classes are held on Zoom. Your lesson reminder email arrives 36 hours before class and contains the Zoom link. If Zoom prompts for a password, it is Kafka.

💡 What should I do to prepare for my lesson?

One of the greatest challenges for me in tutoring is being prepared to teach content that is relevant and specifically tailored to the needs of your teacher and your class. I teach many students from many districts each week, and the needs of two different students can vary greatly. The more we can stay in communication about your class content, the more effective our sessions will be.

Please send me the following before our class session begins:

  • A quick note about what topics you'd like to cover, or what you've been working on in class recently.
  • Any changes to your personal achievement goals.
  • Any documents or files that would be useful for us to look over together: exam study topic lists, worksheets, homework, textbook pages, review guides, and so on. When possible, please send original PDF files, as these are easier for me to work with during class than images.

Photos taken with your phone are perfectly fine. Please avoid sending links unless they are publicly viewable (you can check this by opening the link in an incognito window).

Can I record our lessons?

Yes! Click the record button in Zoom during a lesson to begin recording. The video file is saved to your own computer at the end of the meeting, for your personal use.

Do you assign homework?

Homework assignments are available by request and are generally not provided by default. Under most circumstances they're free of charge.

Students are often very busy, so additional tutor-assigned homework isn't always appropriate. If it would be helpful, just let me know roughly how much time you can reasonably devote between classes and I'll put something together.

Are progress reports available?

Yes. Progress reports are available on request for parents of youth students taking at least two lessons per month, up to one report per month. Email me at to request one.

What should I have ready for class?

A quiet space, a reliable internet connection, and a way to show me your written work (a phone camera, a tablet, or simply holding your paper up to the webcam all work). I'll share a digital whiteboard during class, so you don't need any special software beyond Zoom.
